FEATURES

What LyricsStatus handles.

The essentials for keeping Spotify playback and your Discord status together.

LIVE LYRICS

Lyrics follow playback.

The current synced line updates as the song moves, using Spotify playback from your Discord connection.

CONNECTION

No Spotify API keys.

LyricsStatus uses the Spotify account already linked to your Discord profile.

STATUS RESTORE

Keeps your saved status.

When playback stops or the app closes, your saved Discord status is restored automatically.
